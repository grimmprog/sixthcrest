Case Study: Bananaz Fashion WooCommerce to Shopify Migration (+55% Mobile Sales Growth)

Client: Bananaz Fashion (bananaz.in) WooCommerce to Shopify Migration August 2026

1. The Client Background

Bananaz Fashion is a pioneering eco-luxury apparel brand specializing in high-end clothing and accessories crafted from 100% natural organic banana fibre. Serving eco-conscious luxury buyers in India and internationally, Bananaz requires high-resolution image galleries and smooth multi-currency checkout.

2. The Problem: Unscalable WooCommerce Infrastructure

Bananaz was originally launched on WooCommerce. As international traffic and catalog variations grew, their WordPress server hit major performance walls:

  • Frequent Database Lockups: PHP memory limits caused database crashes during flash sale spikes.
  • Complex Plugin Bloat: Over 35 active WordPress plugins created severe security vulnerabilities and slow 5.2s page loads.
  • Broken International Payments: WooCommerce currency conversion plugins created checkout errors for international buyers.

3. SixthCrest Migration & Engineering Solution

SixthCrest engineered a complete zero-downtime migration from WooCommerce to Shopify:

  • Automated Data Migration: Migrated 100% of product SKUs, customer records, and order history without losing SEO link equity or customer passwords.
  • Bespoke Liquid Theme: Built a lightweight Shopify theme highlighting sustainable banana fibre storytelling with interactive fabric closeups.
  • Native Shopify Markets Integration: Enabled multi-currency pricing (USD, EUR, GBP, INR) with localized checkout flows.
