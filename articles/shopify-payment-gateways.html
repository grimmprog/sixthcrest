Shopify Payment Gateways: Stripe, Razorpay, UPI & One-Click Checkout CRO

Written by SixthCrest Growth Team 7 Min Read August 2026

Over 68% of e-commerce shopping carts are abandoned right at the payment step. Providing local payment methods, zero-friction express checkouts, and multi-currency billing is essential to driving revenue.

1. Selecting the Right Payment Gateways

Depending on your target market, offering localized payment methods increases checkout completion rates by up to 35%:

  • United States / Europe / Global: Stripe, Shopify Payments, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal Express.
  • India / South Asia: Razorpay, PayU, PhonePe, Paytm, and Instant UPI Intent flow.
  • Latin America & LATAM: Mercado Pago & local installments.

2. Integrating Instant UPI & One-Click Checkout for India

For Indian e-commerce stores (like Bananaz and Politico Fit), standard credit card checkout forms see high drop-offs. Integrating Instant UPI Intent (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm) allows buyers to approve payments directly on their mobile phones in under 5 seconds without typing card numbers or waiting for SMS OTPs.
