Case Study: Politico Fit Luxury Menswear Storefront (+42% CRO Increase)

Client: Politico Fit (politico.fit) Shopify E-Commerce Redesign & CRO August 2026

1. The Client & Market Background

Politico Fit is a high-end luxury menswear fashion brand headquartered in Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad. Known for bespoke tailored suits, bandhgalas, and handcrafted luxury attire, the brand wanted to expand its digital reach across North America, UAE, and India.

2. The Business Challenge

Despite strong physical brand equity, Politico Fit's initial e-commerce store suffered from key conversion bottlenecks:

  • Generic Store Aesthetics: The off-the-shelf theme failed to communicate the craftsmanship and luxury price point of high-end menswear.
  • Sizing Uncertainty: High cart abandonment due to customers being unsure about custom suit fit measurements.
  • Mobile Performance Issues: Heavy uncompressed images resulted in a slow 4.8s mobile load time, dropping mobile conversion rate to under 1.2%.

3. SixthCrest Engineering Solution

SixthCrest re-engineered Politico Fit's entire Shopify ecosystem from the ground up:

  • Bespoke Liquid Theme Architecture: Developed a light, dark-mode compatible custom Liquid theme tailored specifically for high-end fashion storytelling.
  • Interactive Size Fitting Engine: Built a custom JavaScript fit recommendation wizard that guides buyers to input height, chest, and shoulder measurements for personalized fit recommendations.
  • Sub-Second Ajax Cart Drawer: Implemented a slide-out cart drawer with dynamic upsells, free shipping threshold progress bar, and instant express checkout triggers.
  • Core Web Vitals Speed Optimization: Converted images to Next-Gen WebP, lazy-loaded off-screen assets, and streamlined JavaScript bundles to achieve 95+ mobile performance score.
