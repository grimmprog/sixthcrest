Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Sixthcrest ("we," "our," or "us") operates the website https://sixthcrest.com and our associated mobile applications, including RxDoctor. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our apps and website, we may collect:

  • Personal Data: Name, email address, phone number, or professional details provided voluntarily during registration or support requests.
  • Health & Medical Data (App-Specific): If applicable within specific health applications (such as RxDoctor), any health information provided is processed solely to deliver the app’s core functionality to you.
  • Device & Usage Data: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and anonymous diagnostic logs to maintain performance and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our mobile applications and services.
  • Process requests, support inquiries, and user interactions.
  • Ensure compliance with developer policies, legal obligations, and security standards.
  • Improve performance, fix bugs, and optimize user experience.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data to third parties. We may disclose information only:

  • To comply with legal obligations or valid legal processes.
  • To protect and defend the rights, property, or safety of Sixthcrest, our users, or the public.
  • With service providers operating under strict confidentiality obligations to support essential infrastructure.

4. Health Data Privacy (RxDoctor)

For applications handling health data (such as RxDoctor):

  • Data is transmitted securely using standard encryption protocols (HTTPS / TLS 1.3).
  • Health data is strictly utilized to provide application features requested by the user and is never used for advertising purposes.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal data held by Sixthcrest. To make a request, contact us at support@sixthcrest.com.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact:

  • Email: support@sixthcrest.com
  • Website: https://sixthcrest.com
